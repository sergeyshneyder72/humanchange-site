Glossary

Working definitions of terms we use to describe our research areas.

Behavioral science
The study of how and why people make decisions and act, drawing on psychology, cognitive science, and economics to understand and predict behavior.
Habit formation
The process by which a behavior becomes automatic through repetition in a consistent context, requiring progressively less conscious effort to perform.
Digital interventions
Structured programs delivered through software — apps, messaging, or other digital channels — designed to support behavior change at scale.
AI coaching
The use of artificial intelligence to provide personalized, adaptive guidance and feedback in support of a person's goals, informed by their behavior over time.
Long-term adherence
The extent to which a person sustains a behavior or intervention over an extended period, as distinct from short-term engagement or initial adoption.
