Privacy Policy

Health Capital ("we," "us") is an early-stage research and technology initiative. This policy describes what happens to your data when you use humanchange.app and the Health Capital app.

What stays on your device

Your onboarding answers (age, sex, region, weight, activity, sleep, nutrition, smoking, alcohol) and your daily habit logs are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your own device. We do not receive, transmit, or store this data on any server — it never leaves your device unless you choose to send it to us yourself, for example by email.

What we do collect

If you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase), so you can sign in. We do not use this email for marketing without your consent.

We use self-hosted, privacy-respecting analytics (Umami) to count anonymous usage events, such as completing onboarding. This does not include any personal information and does not use third-party tracking cookies.

If you email us for support, billing, or feedback, we use your message only to respond to you.

Your choices

Because most of your data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for humanchange.app. To delete your account (email/password) or ask any question about your data, contact us at support@humanchange.app.

Changes

We'll update this page as the product evolves.
