HumanChange

HumanChange is an early-stage research and technology initiative focused on helping people build healthier habits through evidence-based behavioral science and artificial intelligence.

Mission

Our mission is to help people build sustainable, healthy habits by combining behavioral science with artificial intelligence. We aim to complement — not replace — clinical care, and to ground our work in evidence rather than trends.

Research

We are interested in research and collaboration across the following areas:

  • Behavioral science
  • Habit formation
  • Digital interventions
  • AI coaching
  • Long-term adherence

Collaboration

We welcome conversations with researchers, clinicians and institutions interested in advancing evidence-based behavior change.
